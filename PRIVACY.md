# Privacy Policy — Purrfect Frame

Purrfect Frame is a single-player game. It does not require an account and it does not include analytics, advertising, or a backend.

## Data on the device

- Level stars and settings are stored in the app’s Application Support folder.
- Shots you take are stored in that same folder so the in-app collection can show them.
- If you choose **Save to Photos**, the image is written to your photo library. That is the only time the app uses the Photos permission, and it is add-only.

## Data we do not collect

- No name, email, or identifier is sent anywhere.
- No usage analytics.
- No tracking across apps or websites.
